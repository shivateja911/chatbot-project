# Report a phishing or suspicious email
Tags: phishing, suspicious email, scam, security
1. Do not click links, open attachments or reply.
2. Use the "Report phishing" button in your mail client.

If you clicked a link, opened an attachment or entered your password: this is a security incident.
Contact IT security immediately (extension 5555). Change your password at https://reset.lumenharbor.example from a different device. Do not delete the email.
