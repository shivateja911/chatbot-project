# Reset a forgotten or expired password
Tags: password, reset, expired, locked out, login, sign in
1. Go to https://reset.lumenharbor.example from any device.
2. Enter your work email and approve the MFA prompt.
3. Choose a new password of at least 14 characters that you have not used before.
4. Lock and unlock your laptop while connected to the office network or VPN.

An account locks for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts. Waiting 15 minutes unlocks it.
If you cannot approve MFA, see KB-003. IT staff never ask for your password.
