# Add and use an office printer
Tags: printer, printing, print queue, badge
1. Connect to the office network (VPN does not give access to printers).
2. Add the printer named "Harbor-FollowMe" from your system's printer settings.
3. Send your job, then tap your badge at any printer to release it.

Jobs are deleted after 24 hours. If a job does not appear, remove the printer, add it again and resend. For paper jams or toner, open a ticket with the printer's floor and label.
