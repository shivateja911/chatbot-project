# Connect to the company VPN (HarborConnect)
Tags: vpn, remote, harborconnect, network, connection
1. Open HarborConnect and select the "Global" gateway.
2. Sign in with your work email and approve the MFA prompt.

If it fails to connect:
- Confirm that ordinary websites load without the VPN.
- Quit HarborConnect completely, reopen it and try again.
- Restart the laptop.
- Try another network, such as a phone hotspot. Some public Wi-Fi blocks VPN.

If it still fails, open a ticket with the error message, your operating system and the network you used.
